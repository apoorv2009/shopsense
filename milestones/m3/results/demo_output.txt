┌─────────────────────────────────────────────── How do I get a refund for a cash on delivery order? ───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ For cash on delivery orders, refunds are sent by bank transfer to an account in the customer's own name, which is verified before the transfer.   │
│ Customers need to provide the account holder name, account number, and IFSC code securely. Bank transfers are initiated within 7 days of          │
│ receiving the returned item and typically reach the account within 3 to 5 business days. Alternatively, store credit can be offered.              │
│ citations: cash-on-delivery#1, cash-on-delivery#2, cash-on-delivery#3                                                                             │
