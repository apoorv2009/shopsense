# General return policy

## 1. Return window
Most products bought on ShopSense can be returned within **30 days** of delivery. The window is counted in calendar days from the delivery date (day 1 is the day after delivery). A return is accepted through the app or by writing to support on or before day 30.

Categories with a different window (electronics, accessories, apparel, home and kitchen, beauty) follow their own section and take precedence over this general rule.

## 2. Conditions for return
The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with all tags, accessories and invoice. Items returned without the invoice are processed after verification and may take 3 extra working days.

## 3. Refund destination
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. See the refund methods policy for timelines.
