# Goodwill credits and escalation

Goodwill credits recognise inconvenience, such as a late delivery or a poor support experience. They are not refunds for a product.

## 1. Limit
Support can offer a goodwill credit of up to **₹500** per order. The credit is store credit valid for 90 days.

## 2. Above the limit
A goodwill request above ₹500 is escalated to a support lead. The customer is told that the request has been passed on and when to expect a reply.

## 3. Repeated requests
A customer who has received goodwill credits on three or more orders in the last 30 days is reviewed by a support lead before any further credit is offered.
