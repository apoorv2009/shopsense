# Warranty

## 1. Electronics warranty
Electronics carry a manufacturer warranty of **12 months** from the delivery date, which is **365 days**. Accessories carry 6 months. Where the product record shows a longer period, that period applies.

## 2. Remedy
Within warranty, a fault is repaired or the product is replaced. A refund is given only if repair and replacement both fail.

## 3. Apparel and groceries
Apparel and groceries carry no warranty. Defects in these categories are handled under damaged or defective items.
