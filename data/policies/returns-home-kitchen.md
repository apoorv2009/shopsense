# Home and kitchen returns

Cookware, appliances and home textiles follow a shorter window than apparel.

## 1. Return window for home and kitchen
Home and kitchen products can be returned within **10 days** of delivery. Products must not have been used for cooking, and the original seal on non-stick cookware must be intact.

## 2. Bedding
Bedsheets and linen can be returned within the 10-day window if unwashed and in original packaging. Washed or used linen is not returnable unless faulty.

## 3. Faulty appliances
A faulty appliance is handled under damaged or defective items and, after the 10-day window, under the warranty policy.
