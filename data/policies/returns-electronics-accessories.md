# Electronics accessories addendum

Accessories are small electronic add-ons such as power banks, chargers, cables and cases. They have a shorter window than main electronics. This section overrides both the electronics addendum and the general policy.

## 1. Return window for accessories
Electronic accessories can be returned within **7 days** of delivery. Day 7 is the last accepted day; a return requested on day 8 is not eligible.

## 2. Hygiene and sealing
Chargers and earphones that have been connected to a device cannot be returned for change of mind unless they are faulty. A faulty accessory is handled under damaged or defective items.

## 3. Power banks
Power banks sealed in the original packaging can be returned within the 7-day window. Opened power banks are returnable only if faulty.
